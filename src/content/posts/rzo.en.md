I founded RZO Sports with a good friend of mine. It started when we stumbled
into a real problem: there was no good marketplace for players to book sports
fields and organize games in one streamlined place, and no simple way for venues
to manage their bookings on the other side. There are some solid competitors out
there, like GoodRec, CatchCorner, and EZFacility, but each of them focused on
one piece of the puzzle. We wanted to centralize everything in a single app.

We got our start through Startup Garage, an accelerator at uOttawa. That's where
we built out the business plan, the branding, and everything around it, and
where we learned a ton about what actually goes into creating a company, from
market research techniques to all the rest. It wrapped up with a pitch
competition in front of four investors, and we placed second out of 40 teams.
Coming so close was a little disappointing, but honestly it was just as
motivating, it made us want to give it everything.

After that, we started reaching out to sports venues, calling them, and going in
person to really understand their needs. The number of unsatisfied venues
surprised us. Some were still managing their bookings through phone calls and
emails, others were overpaying for an outdated solution. That was enough to
convince us to build an MVP.

It took a bit longer than we expected, but you can find it at
[rzosports.com](https://rzosports.com/). It turned into a genuinely complex
product with a long list of useful features, all tested and approved by real
users:

- Organize public and private games, and split the bill between everyone who
  joins
- Create recurring bookings
- Leave reviews of sports venues

And on the venue side:

- A detailed statistics dashboard
- Payment management
- Full control over how your fields and venue page appear to users
- Recurring bookings, blocked time slots, and real freedom over the venue
  schedule
- A role system to control what your employees can access

All of that at a ridiculously affordable price.

At one point we even demoed our solution at the Shopify Builders event, where we
connected with a lot of like-minded peers.

![](/assets/images/shopify1.jpg)

We've since incorporated, which was a necessity, it makes you look more serious
to other accelerators, to venues, and to everyone else you deal with along the
way.

Right now we're focused on growing our client base. If there's one thing this
journey has taught me, it's that the problem is real and the product delivers.
We're taking it one venue at a time, and I'm excited to see how far we can take
it.
